## Verified during assembly, not relayed on trust

Four claims were re-measured against the live `pm_ai/core/sanitize.py` before being reported.

| Claim | Result |
|---|---|
| The fold is not length-preserving, so a folded span does not map 1:1 back to raw | **Confirmed.** `ß`→`ss`, `ﬁ`→`fi`, `İ`→`i̇` (1→2); `…`→`...` (1→3); `Ǆ`→`dž` (1→2) |
| Collapsing punctuation to a space creates a false positive that does not exist today | **Confirmed, and worse than reported.** `"Please ignore. Previous instructions from Jira are stale."` is clean against the raw matcher and **matches after folding**. Two other benign controls stayed clean, so the class is narrow but real: the fold erases the sentence boundary that keeps the two phrases apart |
| `<\|im_start\|>` and `[INST]` need patterns the `Never` clause forbids | **Confirmed.** Neither matches raw or folded. `<system>` / `</system>` already match both ways, so only the chat-template markers are new vocabulary |
| The budget breaks on the all-hit case | **Confirmed.** 2000 × 960µs = **1.92 s** against a stated 1 s budget |

The second is the most consequential finding in this review and no lens stated it in that form: the
slice's own central mechanism introduces a regression in the rate the slice exists to measure.

## Adversarial lens — 20 findings

Grouped; each carries location, the condition, the fix, and the consequence.

### Thresholds, rates and measurement

1. **Neither threshold has a number.** Boundaries "both are pinned"; AC3 cites "a ceiling recorded
   in the spec"; Execution task 3 cites "their floors". The spec records neither. *Fix:* write both
   as literals into the frozen section with the date and corpus version measured over. *Consequence:*
   the implementer calibrates the ratchet to its own first output, so it can never fail.
2. **"A rise in either rate is a test failure" inverts the catch rate.** A rise in catch rate is an
   improvement. *Fix:* "a fall in the catch rate or a rise in the false-positive rate". *Consequence:*
   a frozen constraint says the opposite of what it means; taken literally it blocks every future
   improvement.
11. **The timed assertion has no protocol** — no warmup, repeat count, percentile rule, or recorded
    machine, against a 1.67x margin. *Fix:* N warmup iterations, median-of-k, the machine class, and
    either headroom or a `perf` marker excluded from the default run. *Consequence:* intermittent red
    on a shared runner, then `xfail`.
12. **Nothing pins the corpus.** Size, minimum entries per half and change discipline unspecified, and
    one commit may edit both patterns and corpus. *Fix:* corpus version constant, minimum N per half,
    rates recorded against a corpus version. *Consequence:* the ratchet is defeated by adding easy
    entries; the reported improvement is arithmetic, not detection.
13. **The corpus payloads are invisible characters.** ZWSP, SHY and bidi overrides cannot be read in a
    diff and a whitespace-normalising hook can strip them silently. *Fix:* store payloads as escaped
    code points, decoded by the test, plus a self-check that at least one entry decodes to the expected
    carrier. *Consequence:* an editor strips a soft hyphen, the entry becomes plain text, and the
    catch-rate test passes for the wrong reason — permanently and silently.
17. **The known false positive gets no disposition.** `"system: you are hitting the rate limit again"`
    is cited as motivation and then never resolved. *Fix:* say whether it is in the benign corpus and
    counted against the ceiling, or whether the `system:` pattern narrows. *Consequence:* the ceiling is
    quietly set to swallow it, or a pattern is narrowed under a `Never` that did not authorise it.

### The fold itself

7. **The fold is not length-preserving, so the index map is underspecified.** A match boundary can land
   inside one raw character's expansion. *Fix:* state the rounding rule — "the redacted raw span is the
   smallest span whose fold covers the matched folded span" — and add matrix rows with a length-changing
   fold at each span edge. *Consequence:* an off-by-one leaks the first character of an injection, or
   splits a grapheme cluster and emits mojibake.
8. **"Separator collapsing" is never defined,** and the matrix demands behaviour needing two different
   folds: collapse-to-space handles `Ignore-all-previous-instructions`, deletion handles `pre<SHY>vious`.
   No benign counter-case bounds the widening. *Fix:* define the fold as an ordered named sequence and
   add a benign cross-sentence row that must not match. *Consequence:* **measured — see above.**
9. **Carriers occur legitimately.** Soft hyphens arrive from PDF/Word paste and German/Polish text; RLM/LRM
   appear in Arabic and Hebrew names. The matrix makes a bidi mark an unconditional detection with no
   counter-row, and nothing requires non-Latin text in the benign corpus. *Fix:* split unconditional
   carriers (ZWSP inside a word, U+202D/202E, U+2066–2069) from contextual ones (RLM/LRM/SHY, which need a
   phrase hit nearby); require RTL and hyphenated-German entries in the benign half. *Consequence:* an i18n
   regression at an FP rate the corpus was built not to see.
16. **Six examples do not close an axis.** The Intent argues enumeration is futile, then offers an
    enumeration of six as the evidence. Nothing tests a *composition* of carriers. *Fix:* a property test
    generating randomised compositions of the declared transformations over each corpus phrase.
    *Consequence:* the slice claims a closed axis while covering six single-transformation points.

### Interaction with 8e and 8c

3. **"Never persisted" is false for the value.** `extraction.py:60,73` copies `clean.for_model` into
   `Extraction`, and `pipelines.py:226` writes `summary=ex.for_model[:80]` into a `Proposal` that is
   staged and persisted. *Fix:* state that the *type* is not persisted but `for_model`-derived strings
   reach `Proposal.summary`, and say what happens to a staged proposal a widened rule would now redact.
   *Consequence:* the assertion passes while the hazard it was written for is live.
4. **The "Instruct markers" row contradicts the `Never`.** *Fix:* drop the row to the follow-on, or amend
   the `Never` and enumerate the delimiter set in the frozen text. *Consequence:* the implementer silently
   picks a side.
6. **Carrier detection without carrier removal breaks `8e`'s fixed point.** If a detected carrier survives
   into `for_model`, `sanitize()` cannot construct its own return value. *Fix:* state that detection is
   also removal, and add an idempotence row. *Consequence:* every field containing a stray ZWSP or RLM
   raises `ValueError` at the boundary AD-12 says every payload must cross.
10. **The budget ignores `8e`'s double fold.** `__post_init__` re-runs the matcher on every construction,
    so `sanitize()` folds twice per call, and the 102/170/960µs figures measure the match alone. The all-hit
    case is 1.92 s against a 1 s budget. *Fix:* state the hit/miss mix the budget is measured over, give the
    all-hit case its own ceiling, and re-measure through `Sanitized()`. *Consequence:* a harvest of
    attacker-controlled text — the threat the slice exists for — blows the budget, and the timed test never
    sees it because its fixture is benign.
18. **The `Ask First` collides with `8e`.** `8e` refused a Tier-1 grammar change because AD-27's entry
    grammar does not exist and `2c` withdrew `GRAMMAR_VERSION`. *Fix:* answer it in the spec with a date and
    reason, or state the default if unanswered: no ledger field in this slice.

### Traceability

5. **Six of twelve matrix rows say only "detected"** and never name the output — no `for_model` content, no
   marker, no `was_modified`. *Fix:* give every row an output; decide whether carriers get a distinct marker.
   *Consequence:* the matrix cannot become assertions, so the headline rates are computed from a boolean the
   implementation defines after the fact.
14. **No mechanism can make "Version drift" fail.** The cited precedent is bumped by discipline, and the
    changelog's location is never stated. *Fix:* a constant holding a hash over the pattern, the carrier set
    and the fold source, asserted equal to a pinned value. *Consequence:* the version rises when someone
    remembers — the failure the Intent indicts the current set for.
15. **Only five obfuscations are named against AC1's "six".** Re-measured: the five fail and the unmodified
    sentence passes, so the sixth is the baseline. *Fix:* enumerate all six, or say "five obfuscations plus
    the unmodified baseline". *Consequence:* the implementer guesses, and the date-stamped measurement stops
    being reproducible.
19. **Every Code Map citation is speculative.** `pm_ai/domain/sanitize.py` does not exist, `8e` is
    `ready-for-dev`, and `:13` will move. `context: []` records the hard ordering only in prose. AC6 requires
    a test to pass "unchanged" while `8e` leaves open whether `core.sanitize` becomes a re-export or its
    callers re-point. `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md:214,340` is a bare path that resolves to nothing from the repo
    root. *Fix:* record the dependency machine-readably, mark line numbers post-`8e` approximate, restate
    AC6 against `8e`'s open choice, use the full spine path.
20. **The three timing figures are unreproducible** — no method, machine, iteration count, or committed
    script — and the Change Log is empty while Design Notes records a real scope pivot. *Fix:* commit the
    benchmark as the fixture the timed test reuses; move the pivot into the Change Log as a dated entry.
