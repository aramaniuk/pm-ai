- (direction) Open question 4 resolved by the user, 2026-08-22. The sleeping laptop and the broken connector ARE distinguishable, and the distinguishing fact is whether an attempt was made: a sleeping laptop makes no fetch attempts at all, while a broken connector keeps attempting and the attempts appear in the logs marked failed. Collapsing both into UNKNOWN was the mistake. A new commitment state ERROR carries the second case.
- (constraint) An overdue commitment resolves by what pm-ai actually attempted, not only by what it found. Three outcomes, never collapsed: a harvest that succeeded across the arrival window and found no admissible evidence is BROKEN; no successful harvest spanning that window with no failed attempt is UNKNOWN; any contributing instance that attempted and failed over that window is ERROR.
- (constraint) UNKNOWN is temporary and self-resolving - it means pm-ai has not yet had a complete look, and it resolves to FULFILLED or BROKEN once coverage catches up. No human action is implied by it.
- (constraint) ERROR is not self-resolving. It names a harvest that cannot succeed until a human acts - expired token, revoked consent, moved repository, broken configuration - and is surfaced with the failing instance named, in pm-ai doctor and the briefing. Waiting never clears it.
- (constraint) ERROR outranks UNKNOWN when both apply: an instance that is actively failing is the actionable fact, and one that merely did not run is not.
- (constraint) Neither UNKNOWN nor ERROR may trigger a pre-meeting inquiry to the person who made the promise. pm-ai's own blindness is never someone else's accountability: absence of data is not evidence, pre-meeting inquiries cannot be recalled, and the alert goes privately to the PM naming the instrument rather than the promise-owner.
- (constraint) A coverage window is a receipt for a harvest that demonstrably succeeded, never a span computed from the clock. A harvest that ran without fetching reports no coverage and records its attempt as failed, so never-looked and looked-and-failed stay distinguishable - the distinction all three verdicts rest on.
- (note) Finding recorded against stories 8 and 9, surfaced while resolving open question 4: pm_ai/connectors/gitlab.py:62-71 builds its CoverageWindow as now-minus-4h to now, computed from the clock and never tied to whether a fetch succeeded, and run_harvest in pm_ai/app/pipelines.py has no try/except. So a loud failure (connector raises) skips save_cursor and records no coverage, while a quiet failure (provider answers with an empty 200) records FULL coverage and would drive an overdue commitment to BROKEN. The two failure modes produce opposite verdicts, decided by how politely the provider declines. Added, with mkdir and chmod deliberately left out and the reason recorded: creating a directory is not opening a file for writing, and the resolver legitimately creates scope directories for the writer to write into.
