- (constraint) The LLM core never receives shell or raw terminal privileges; every model-driven change to external state routes through a registry-authorized MCP skill. Read-only harvesting, frontier calls, and local model subprocesses are separately classified.
- (constraint) Input sanitization is non-destructive: it produces a derived copy for model context while the raw payload is retained under the retention policy, so citations and drift checks still resolve against the true source.
- (constraint) Spoken commands auto-execute only when all three hold - provider-authenticated source with provider-issued speaker identity, speaker resolves to the PM, and the verb is reversible AND non-notifying. Any condition unmet stages.
- (constraint) Irreversible verbs (outbound email or DM, MR/PR creation, closures, deletions) always stage regardless of source or speaker: one-tap undo cannot recall a notification.
- (constraint) Manually supplied transcripts are never an auto-execution source, and every ingested transcript must bind to a meeting record.
- (constraint) Personal-scope material never reaches employer-controlled systems. Frontier model APIs are a disclosed, logged exception; the boundary also binds destination - personal-scope material may never enter a prompt whose output is bound for a project artifact or external system.
- (constraint) The disclosure and cost ledger is application-scoped at ~/.pm-ai/disclosure.md, never a per-scope event log and never committed - otherwise the audit mechanism becomes the leak.
- (constraint) Only externally-authored telemetry is admissible as commitment evidence. Every event carries an authorship marker of external, pm-ai, or unknown; pm-ai's own writes and unknown are inadmissible.
- (constraint) An overdue commitment with no telemetry coverage for its window resolves to [UNKNOWN], never [BROKEN]: absence of data is not evidence, and inquiries cannot be recalled.
- (constraint) All Markdown in every scope is plaintext by design - transparency over one's own record is a product property. The encrypted set is defined and closed; derived state is unencrypted and rebuildable.
- (constraint) The daemon binds loopback only, exposes zero public ports, and reaches Telegram by outbound HTTPS long-polling. Webhooks are prohibited: they require the public endpoint this same rule forbids.
- (constraint) Local workloads run on an 8B-class instruct model at Q4_K_M plus Whisper small.en; minimum hardware is 16GB Apple Silicon and v1 is macOS-only. Models above 8B-class are out of scope for v1.
- (constraint) The $20/month cost figure is a monitored target, not a cap. Breach warns; the system never silently degrades output, downgrades models, or disables features.
- (constraint) Self-improvement operates inside the architecture, never on it. The tunable surface is closed to persona, retrieval weighting, and memory patterns; pm-ai may not alter its skill registry, declared permissions, egress classification, or scope boundaries.
- (constraint) pm-ai does not generate code - not to execute, not to test, not as a diff for review. It names capability gaps in prose and a human writes the skill.
- (constraint) Retrieval weighting changes ordering, never the record. Ledgers stay append-only; bounded forgetting is recorded compaction, never silent decay.
- (constraint) An adaptation is refused when ratings rise while challenge falls (question ratio, blind spots surfaced, experiments proposed), and refused on the retrieval axis when novelty declines as engagement rises.
- (constraint) Alignment lifts but never overrides: an aligned task outranks an unaligned one at comparable urgency, and an unaligned production incident still outranks a long-term refactor.
- (constraint) Unaligned work is never hidden - it ranks lower, stays individually visible, and is surfaced as a set. Nothing may be marked aligned in order to promote it; rank follows a resolvable goal citation.
- (constraint) A task the alignment engine cannot align renders as UNALIGNED, never untagged.
- (constraint) Raw transcripts are purged after 30 days (configurable) once verified conversion into summaries, work item updates, and decision logs has occurred.
- (constraint) Recovery is tier-scoped: Truth and Operational state are backup targets and survive; only Derived state is disposable and rebuildable with zero loss. Operational state is never a rebuild target.
- (constraint) Scope is decided by subject, not convenience: a meeting record and its transcript live in the scope that owns the meeting, and a committed record may cite only a meeting in its own scope.
- (constraint) The daemon verifies the transcripts/ gitignore rule before writing a capture and refuses if absent - the rule is the only thing keeping verbatim minutes out of the team repository.
- (constraint) A report's performance record is never readable by that report's peers: custom metrics and dossiers live in the team-member scope, never the sovereign personal scope and never a committed project scope.
- (constraint) Documentation drift checks never block a developer merge request.
